# Youthink Europe
> Youthink Europe is a project founded in Europe, dedicated to youth. Find volunteering, Model UN, youth councils, internships and fellowships around the world. Build a profile of your civic experience, and connect with organizations that share your values.

> [!For Young People]
> A profile that shows your real impact, a feed matched to your causes, and one place to track every application.
> [!For Organizations]
> Reach motivated young people in 190+ countries. Verified organizations can post opportunities and review applicants.

> [!Please note that]
> The project itself is vibe-coded and is sometimes modified by a "human" on Visual Studio Code, softwares such as Claude, and OpenCode have helped for this project to come true.
